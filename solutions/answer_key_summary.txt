Composition of the synthetic dataset
========================================

Seed: 20260818
Genes: 4200
Samples: 16 (8 per condition)
Batches: A and B, balanced within each condition

Differentially expressed genes: 200
  increased in treated: 120
  decreased in treated: 80
  absolute log2 fold change between 0.8 and 3.0
  90 of them use recognizable real gene symbols
  and were given the larger effect sizes on purpose

Gene sets containing an excess of differential genes:
  INFLAMMATORY_RESPONSE: 26 of 43 members
  TNFA_SIGNALING_VIA_NFKB: 32 of 57 members
  HYPOXIA: 31 of 97 members
  DNA_REPAIR: 31 of 116 members

Batch effect: per-gene log2 shift, standard deviation 0.80,
applied to batch B, plus a 12 per cent reduction in depth.
